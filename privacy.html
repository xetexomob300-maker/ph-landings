Privacy Policy

This policy describes how we process the personal data of users who fill in the order form on this site, in line with applicable data protection law.

Data we collect

We collect only what is needed to fulfil the order: your name and phone number.

Purpose

The data is used solely to contact you by phone, confirm the order and deliver it.

Sharing

The data is passed securely only to the customer service team fulfilling the order and is not used for marketing without your consent.

Retention

The data is kept for as long as needed to fulfil the order and handle any complaints.

Your rights

You may access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data; write to us at the address on the Contact page.
